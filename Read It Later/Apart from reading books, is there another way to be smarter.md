For your information : Even books are not enough to be an intelligent person.

Books contain information, just like other media (website, YouTube, social media, games). From news and discussions we can also get information.

If the collection of information is arranged correctly it will become knowledge (knowledge).

But it takes more than that to be smart. Because intelligence contains the most important characteristics, namely: solution.

Smart people are people who have solutions/answers so they can provide them insight for others.

Experience, strategy, intuition and creativity so additional provisions to become an intelligent person.

![](https://qph.cf2.quoracdn.net/main-qimg-21e27939a017e952fa97d35f2f8562cc)

Tips for leveling up at 5 points to be smart are:

1. Knowledge : learn something with the aim of teaching to someone else.
2. Experience : dare to get out of your comfort zone and are not afraid of failure. Failing is fine, as long as you get it lesson learned.
3. Strategy : study systematic and how to analyze, for example simple ones such as SWOT analysis, PDCA, Strategic Initiatives, and fish bone.
4. Intuition : in all processes learn best practices or rule of thumbs. When something goes wrong, we can detect it fast through this intuition. Quickly become a keyword.
5. Creativity : see a problem with perspective different. Or add new elements that are not in the information that has been collected so far.

For your Action : All of that can be trained on Quora.

1. Knowledge: reading other people's answers to explain them to others.
2. Experience: answering questions on a mastered topic and even trying on a new topic
3. Strategy: make critical comments on other people's answers that are inaccurate/wrong with the analysis results
4. Intuition: review of one's own answers, comparison of various answers, detection of misleading negative answers. Upvote, downvote or report answer.
5. Creativity: answering strange questions in a positive style, answering 'if' questions.

Good luck!
